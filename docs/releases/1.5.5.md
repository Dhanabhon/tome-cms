# TomeCMS 1.5.5

Date: 2026-10-01
Status: Stable release; tagged and published as a GitHub release

TomeCMS 1.5.5 adds a rail of a post's headings beside it in Paper, a "New part" break in the editor, a
"Copy link" item on each published post and page, and fixes how the admin's lists open. `/api/v1` is
unchanged.

## Upgrading

- **A managed install** updates from "System" in the admin, as
  [Updating](https://dhanabhon.github.io/tome-cms/running/updating/) describes. There is no
  migration and no new setting to fill in, so the updater backs up the database alone.
- **A build from source** upgrades in place by running the deploy helper again.

`deploy/cloud-init.yaml` now installs 1.5.5.

## Paper: where the reader is

"Reading progress bar" becomes **Reading position**, with three choices: Off, Bar at the top (the bar
as before) and **Rail at the side**. A site keeps what it had: a saved "on" is the bar, and a site that
never chose keeps none.

The rail is a column of short lines at the side of a post, one for each heading, longer for a section
and shorter for a point inside it. The line for the part being read is darker; hovering or focusing
one shows its heading, and choosing it goes there. It appears on a post with at least two headings;
below 64rem, where there is no room beside the text, the bar shows instead. Each heading gets an
address of its own (`#…`), Thai headings included, so a section can be linked to.

## The editor: a new part

The "+" menu has **New part**, a break between two parts of a post, drawn as three dots in the editor,
in Paper and in Plain. A post that already had a horizontal rule shows the same dots.

## The admin

- **Copy link.** The menu on a published post or page has "Copy link", which copies its full public
  address and says "Copied" in place. A draft has no public address yet, so it has no such item.
- **Lists open to fit.** A list opened from the admin's own select, such as the language filter on
  Posts and Pages, is at least as wide as its button and wide enough for its longest choice, lines up
  with a button at the end of its row, and shades only the row the pointer or the keyboard is on; the
  chosen one keeps its check. The language filter no longer draws a line under itself like a tab.

## The README

The logo and the architecture picture at the top of the README follow GitHub's theme rather than the
reader's system setting, so neither disappears on GitHub's light theme under a dark system.

## Testing

- `tests/unit/reading-rail.test.ts` covers heading addresses (English, Thai, repeats, empty headings,
  the ids the page already uses) and when a rail is drawn; `tests/unit/popover.test.ts` covers list
  width and end alignment.
- End-to-end: the rail marks the heading being read, including after an instant jump and at the end
  of a post, and gives way to the bar on a phone; New part is inserted and published; Copy link copies
  a live post's address and is absent on a draft; the language filter's list shows its choices whole
  with one shaded row.
