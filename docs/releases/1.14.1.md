# TomeCMS 1.14.1

Date: 2026-10-04
Status: Stable release; tagged and published as a GitHub release

TomeCMS 1.14.1 gives the themes' reading columns back their width. 1.14.0 held a post's running
text to a narrower measure than its column, and on a wide screen that read as a page that stopped
short. `/api/v1` is unchanged.

## Upgrading

- **A managed install** updates from "System" in the admin, as
  [Updating](https://dhanabhon.github.io/tome-cms/running/updating/) describes, or with
  `sudo tome update` on the server. There is no migration, so the updater backs up the database
  alone.
- **A build from source** upgrades in place by running the deploy helper again.
- **The updater** stays at 1.6.0. Nothing to upgrade on the server.

`deploy/cloud-init.yaml` now installs 1.14.1.

## Fixed

- **Paper:** a post's paragraphs, lists, quotations and headings span the post's column, as far as
  its cover and pictures, instead of stopping at 33rem.
- **Plain:**
  - the home page's lead title fills the line before it wraps, instead of being balanced into two
    short lines, and its summary spans the page;
  - the post grid fits as many 18rem columns as there is room for, and the posts there share the
    whole width: two posts on a wide screen are halves, not two thirds and an empty third;
  - a post's running text spans its column, as its pictures, code and file cards do.
- **Almanac:** a post, and a notice page, is 44em wide (about 790 pixels) instead of 35em, still
  centred.

## For theme authors

Nothing changed in the theme contract. A theme that wants a narrower measure can still set one on
its own body text.

## Testing

- **Unit:** the theme tests now check that Paper's and Plain's post text carries no width cap,
  that Plain's grid fits columns to the room, and that Almanac's column is 44em.
- **Browser:** the theme screenshots in English and Thai, light and dark, at 320 to 1440 pixels
  wide.
