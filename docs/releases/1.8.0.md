# TomeCMS 1.8.0

Date: 2026-10-01
Status: Stable release; tagged and published as a GitHub release

TomeCMS 1.8.0 makes an AI app and the owner take turns on a draft. The editor shows when an app has
just read or changed the draft, and an app cannot change a draft while the owner has it open. Claude
and ChatGPT/Codex now show their marks where a connection is named, and a few rough edges from 1.7.0
are smoothed. `/api/v1` is unchanged.

## Upgrading

- **A managed install** updates from "System" in the admin, as
  [Updating](https://dhanabhon.github.io/tome-cms/running/updating/) describes. There is no migration
  and no new setting, so the updater backs up the database alone.
- **A build from source** upgrades in place by running the deploy helper again.

`deploy/cloud-init.yaml` now installs 1.8.0.

## The owner first

1.7.0 never wrote over anything. Both sides send the draft's version, so the later save gets a
conflict. But when an app changed a draft while the owner had it open, it was the owner who lost: the
editor's next autosave failed, and reloading threw away what they had typed.

From 1.8.0 the editor checks in every 15 seconds, while its tab is visible, for any saved draft,
including a new one from its first save. While the check is fresh (45 seconds), an app's change to
that draft is refused. The app is told to ask the owner to close it, or to make a new draft instead.
Reading and new drafts are never refused. Closing the draft or leaving its tab hidden ends the hold,
so nothing stays locked. The check runs twice: when the change arrives, and again just before the
change is written.

## What the editor shows

A bar above the editor says when an app read or changed the draft in the last 3 minutes, for example
"Claude read this draft 1 minute ago. While you have it open, an AI cannot change it." When the stored
draft is newer than the editor's, for example after a change from another tab, it says so and offers
**Load the latest**. When an app has just changed the draft and the **Put back** bar already names
that app, this bar says only the second sentence.

All of this is kept in the app's memory, not the database. A restart forgets at most a few minutes of
it.

## Marks for Claude and ChatGPT

Claude's mark and OpenAI's mark (from thesvg.org, unmodified) now appear on the consent screen and in
the connections list. A mark is chosen only from something the app cannot choose for itself:

- the address its approval goes back to (`claude.ai`, `chatgpt.com`); or
- the host of the client document it identified itself with (`claude.ai`, `chatgpt.com`, `openai.com`,
  `platform.openai.com`).

So Claude Code and Codex, whose approval goes back to your own computer, still get their marks from
their client documents. An app that only *calls itself* Claude gets a computer icon and "(the name it
gave)". An approval going back to your own computer now reads "A program on this computer", with the
address underneath, instead of a bare `127.0.0.1:49205`.

## Fixes from 1.7.0

- **Put back:**
  - keeps the draft's current address if its old one has since been taken by another post;
  - leaves out a category or cover picture that has been deleted, instead of failing.
- **The connections list** says "Reads only while writing is switched off" when "Allow AI to write
  drafts" is off.
- **OAuth tidying:**
  - expired codes and tokens are removed;
  - a connection with no token left, after a day, is revoked;
  - an app with no live connection is removed;
  - when too many apps are registered, the message says to revoke unused ones on the Plugins screen.
- **Logs:** each write and fault line from an app carries a request id. Content is still never logged.

  - the poll gate and the one-at-a-time guard.
- **Integration:**
  - an app's change refused while the owner is editing, for posts and pages, for any spelling of the
    id, and when the owner opens the draft mid-write;
  - the editing route, scoped to the owner;
  - Put back after a slug clash or a deleted category;
  - OAuth tidying, and the connections list while writing is off.
- **Browser:** the bar after an app reads; a change refused while the editor is open, including for a
  draft just created in this session; the change going through after the editor is closed; Put back
  and Revoke.
- **On daedalus:** the marks with the real Claude and Codex clients.
