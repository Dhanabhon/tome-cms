# TomeCMS 1.12.1

Date: 2026-10-03
Status: Stable release; tagged and published as a GitHub release

TomeCMS 1.12.1 fixes what a design review of the three themes and the admin found broken, and sets
Thai headings with the room their marks need. `/api/v1` is unchanged.

## Upgrading

- **A managed install** updates from "System" in the admin, as
  [Updating](https://dhanabhon.github.io/tome-cms/running/updating/) describes, or with
  `sudo tome update` on the server. There is no migration, so the updater backs up the database
  alone.
- **A build from source** upgrades in place by running the deploy helper again.
- **The updater** stays at 1.5.0. Nothing to upgrade on the server.

`deploy/cloud-init.yaml` now installs 1.12.1.

## Fixed

**Themes.**
- In Plain, the default theme:
  - a post's or page's headings now look like headings, not body text;
  - bulleted and numbered lists show their bullets and numbers again;
  - inline code stands out from the words around it.
- A missing or unavailable page reads in Thai on a Thai address, in every theme.
- Paper's post cards no longer show a stray "·" where the date wraps on a phone.

**The admin.**
- The count beside a screen's title, as in "Themes 3", is small again rather than as large as the
  title.
- The Plugins and Themes grids fit a 320-pixel screen.
- A plugin's switch:
  - shows its knob clearly when off, in light and dark;
  - shows that it is saving;
  - answers hover and press.

**Thai type.**
- Headings in the admin and the themes have line spacing tall enough that tone marks and vowels on
  two lines do not touch, and they are not squeezed together.
- Small labels in the admin, such as the sidebar groups, are no longer spaced out letter by letter
  in Thai, and are never smaller than 12 pixels.

## Testing

- **New unit tests:**
  - Plain's headings, lists and inline code;
  - the Thai copy of the missing-page screens;
  - the admin switch;
  - the Thai heading rules;
  - a check that no stylesheet refers to a design token that does not exist, which is how the
    oversized count slipped through.
- **Browser:** the screenshots before and after each fix, in English and Thai, light and dark, and
  at 320 to 1440 pixels wide.
